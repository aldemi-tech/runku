## Large-object boundary

The same Environment exercised both object-transfer classes:

- one bounded 64 MiB Management/Application transfer completed with its checksum intact;
- S3 multipart composed 81 parts into a 5,368,709,121-byte object (5 GiB + 1 byte);
- HEAD returned the exact length and immutable Product version;
- a range GET of the final byte returned exactly one byte;
- multipart listing and abort removed an independent incomplete upload and created no object;
- no incomplete upload remained after completion/abort.

The first 5 GiB direct HTTP attempt exceeded the compact gateway's 35-second request deadline and
the client continued transmitting after the early response, temporarily exhausting the shared
test host. The cell recovered after an EC2 stop/start, no candidate OOM occurred, and production
returned to its exact `0.5.0` digest. This is evidence for the documented split: direct Product and
Console transfers remain bounded, while multi-GiB objects use streamed multipart. It is not a
reason to raise JSON/proxy body limits.
