# Brand identity

Runku's public Product mark is a folded less-than sign (`<`) built from two violet ribbon planes.
Their gradient and restrained native shadow make the fold legible. The form connects the
developer-native language of code with the Product's explicit path back through immutable Releases
and controlled Channel rollback.

The public mark is complete on its own. It must not be wrapped in a cloud, shield, container, or
provider badge. A hosted service may add its own documented operating-plane treatment, but that
treatment does not redefine the Self-Hosted Product or belong to its CLI, SDK, server, or public
release artifacts.

The primary violet is `#856FF0`; Ink is `#18171D`; Paper is `#F7F4ED`; reversed artwork uses
`#FFFFFF`. These transparent PNGs are extracted from the approved Runku H brand board rather than
reconstructed with a generic font or approximate vector geometry. The distinctive cut/notch in
the `k` is intentional and must be preserved. A monochrome reconstruction is not approved yet.

Keep clear space equal to at least one quarter of the mark width. Do not rotate, stretch, outline,
add effects beyond those encoded in the supplied color artwork, recolor by component, close the cut
in the `k`, or combine the mark with another icon. Use the named lockup when the audience may not
yet recognize the standalone mark.

The public copies are byte-identical to their approved Runku Cloud brand masters. Do not recreate
them from screenshots or generic vector geometry.

These files are documentation and communication assets. They do not change any runtime,
distribution, protocol, compatibility, or security contract. The initial identity has not yet
undergone trademark clearance.
