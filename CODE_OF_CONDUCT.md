# Code of Conduct

Contributors must communicate respectfully, evaluate ideas on technical merit, and avoid harassment,
discrimination, threats, or disclosure of private information.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior harms contributors or the
project. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the organization contact
channels.

Expected behavior includes constructive review, patient technical disagreement, attribution,
respect for privacy, and responsible security handling. Harassment, sexualized attention,
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, retaliation, and knowingly unsafe disclosure are not
accepted.

Reports are handled confidentially where practical. Maintainers may warn, moderate, remove content,
restrict interaction, or ban participation based on severity and pattern. Good-faith reporters must
not face retaliation.
